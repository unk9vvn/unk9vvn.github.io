# Multi-factor Authentication

## Check List

* [ ] Verify enforcement of at least two independent factors from different categories: knowledge, possession, or inherence.&#x20;
* [ ] Enumerate supported MFA methods, enrollment flows, recovery options, and alternative authentication paths.&#x20;
* [ ] Test MFA bypass, downgrade, replay, phishing, push-fatigue, SIM-swap, and session-hijacking scenarios.&#x20;
* [ ] Confirm MFA enforcement for privileged, remote, newly created, and high-risk accounts, including step-up authentication.&#x20;
* [ ] Verify secure identity validation for enrollment, reset, replacement, and removal of MFA devices.&#x20;
* [ ] Review MFA configuration responsibilities, centralized policy enforcement, logging, alerting, and continuous monitoring.
